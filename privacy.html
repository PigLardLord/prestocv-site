Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short: PrestoCV collects no data. Your CVs stay on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

PrestoCV is developed by Giovanni Trovato. For any question about this policy or your data, write to support@prestocv.app.

What the app stores, and where

  • The CVs you write, your export settings, and edits you have not saved yet are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage.
  • Like the data of any app, they can be part of your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.
  • Deleting a CV in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

What the app sends

Nothing. PrestoCV makes no network connections, and neither the developer nor any third party receives your CVs or any information about how you use the app.

The AI draft

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, PrestoCV can draft a CV from what you write about yourself. The draft is written by Apple's language model that runs on your device. Your text is not sent to the developer or to any server.

Sharing a PDF

When you export a CV, you choose where the PDF goes through the iOS share sheet: for example Files, Mail or a messaging app. From then on, the app you chose handles the file under its own policy.

Children

PrestoCV is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because PrestoCV collects no personal data, the developer holds nothing about you to access, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control. You can still contact us at the address above, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app ever sends data anywhere, it will ask for your consent first and this policy will explain what is sent and why.
