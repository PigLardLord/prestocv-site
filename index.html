PrestoCV Support

PrestoCV is a free CV and resume maker for iPhone and iPad. Your CVs stay on your device.

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea? Write to support@prestocv.app. Please tell us your device and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Where are my CVs saved?

Only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere. Your device backups can include them, like the data of any app.

What if the app closes before I save?

PrestoCV keeps what you typed. The next time you open the app, it offers to restore your unsaved changes.

How do I get a PDF?

Open a CV and tap Preview & export. Choose a design, then tap Send or save PDF to save it to Files or send it by mail or message.

Why don't I see the AI draft?

The AI draft uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on the device. It needs a supported iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On other devices you can start from a blank CV or from an example.

Which languages are supported?

The app is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese. A CV's headings follow the language the CV is written in, and you can change it before exporting.
